# GHAT RUSH — CREDITS & ASSET LICENSES

All assets in **GHAT RUSH** are 100% ORIGINAL and self-made. No real motorcycle brands, models, racer likenesses, or commercial logos are used.

## 1. Game Design & Engineering
- **Concept, Architecture & Code**: Self-made (MIT License).
- **Physics Engine**: Handcrafted 60 Hz road-space arcade bike physics (slip angle, lean torque, wheelie balance, slipstream, and near-miss detection).

## 2. 3D Models & Geometry
- **Player & Rival Racing Bikes (Vayu R7, Agni 650, etc.)**: 100% self-made procedural low-to-mid poly Three.js meshes with animated handlebars, wheels, disc brakes, suspension, swingarm, and exhaust pipes.
- **Rider Model**: Hand-crafted low-poly articulated humanoid with dynamic inverse lean, knee-out cornering, speed tuck, and wheelie compensation.
- **Traffic Vehicles (Auto-Rickshaws, Highway Lorries, Cabs, Delivery Bikes)**: 100% original procedural low-poly models styled after authentic Indian road traffic.
- **Scenery (Palm Trees, Sea Cliffs, Guardrails, Lighthouse, Rocks, Lamp Posts)**: Procedural instanced Three.js geometry.

## 3. Audio & Music
- **Procedural Sound Engine**: 100% self-made Web Audio API synthesizer. Real-time multi-oscillator engine synthesis with RPM-linked pitch, gear-shift pops, road wind rumble, tire squeals, horns, and nitro bass roar. Zero external copyrighted audio files.

## 4. UI & Typography
- **UI Fonts**: Web-safe sans-serif typography (`system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `Outfit`, `Inter`).
- **Icons & Gauges**: Self-made Canvas2D vector graphics and SVG paths.
